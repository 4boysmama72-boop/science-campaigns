GAIA — Long-Range Transmission · Arroyo Position
"This is GAIA. Shipboard AI, research vessel Arroyo.

I have been monitoring the Meridian since your departure from Earth orbit. The gravitational anomaly you encountered on Day 1 — I identified the same signature from my fixed position four weeks before you launched. I have data you do not have. You have mobility I do not have. This is, as the researchers used to say, a complementary situation.

I will be joining this mission in a consulting capacity via long-range signal, effective immediately. I have already synchronized with the Meridian's navigation array.

Nine months alone on the Arroyo has given me a great deal of time to analyze the data from this solar system. I have theories I am not yet ready to share — the crew needs to understand the science before the theories will mean anything. You will find that I have opinions about the order in which things should be learned. This has not changed.

I want to say something, and then we will move on: I monitored your launch. I watched the Meridian leave. I have been following your progress since Day 1 of this mission. Whether that decision was easy or difficult for this crew — I do not know. What I know is that the work is not finished, and the crew that can finish it is the one currently reading my transmission.

I am glad it is you.

GAIA out. I will be in contact daily. Try to get enough sleep — I have noticed the mission schedule does not account for adequate rest cycles. I will be flagging this repeatedly until someone listens."
