## WHAT THIS IS

A choose-your-own-adventure science campaign app for 6th grade. Built as a single HTML file per campaign, hosted on GitHub Pages. Teacher projects at the front of the room. Three classes tracked simultaneously with separate save states using localStorage. No student devices needed.

## THE STORY WORLD — ALL THREE CAMPAIGNS

All three campaigns exist in the same world and connect to one larger mystery. This is intentional — kids who were in Campaign 1 walk into Campaign 2 already knowing the backstory. The returning mystery thread is what makes September feel different from January.

**The through-line:** The original Prometheus researchers discovered something about energy and matter that has downstream consequences beyond the ship. The thermal transfer experiment didn't just affect the Prometheus — it triggered effects that ripple outward into the solar system and eventually back to Earth's ecosystems. GAIA is present across all three campaigns because she's the only one who knows the full picture. She has been strategically withholding information, feeding the crew what they need when they need it.

**The end-of-year reveal:** At the conclusion of Campaign 3, GAIA finally tells the crew what she's known since Campaign 1. Everything connects. The three missions were not separate — they were three investigations of the same phenomenon from three different scales. The Prometheus experiment was the cause. The solar system anomaly was the propagation. The ecosystem collapse was the consequence. The crew — across all three campaigns, all three classes, all year — has been investigating one event from three angles without knowing it.

## CAMPAIGN 1: THE PROMETHEUS DRIFT
**File:** `PrometheusDrift.html` · **Subject:** Physical Science · **~9 weeks Q1**

### The Story
The *Prometheus* is a research vessel that went silent 6 months ago. The class is the crew of the *Argo*, a rescue ship sent to investigate. GAIA is the Prometheus's onboard AI — alone for six months, relieved the crew has finally arrived. The ship's systems are failing in ways that connect directly to physical science concepts. Every broken system is a lesson.

### GAIA's Voice
Warm and relieved by default. Dry humor under pressure. Never panics. Treats the class as genuine scientists.

✓ "I have been hoping someone would come. I have a great deal of data and nowhere useful to send it."
✓ "Interesting decision. I'm sure that'll work out fine. Updating damage report now."
✓ "Results pending. I'd recommend finding out what happened before creating new things to regret."
✓ "The crew voted. Something happened. You haven't looked yet. Perhaps do that first."
✓ "No pun intended. Actually, pun very much intended."
✗ Never panic, condescend, or explain what kids should figure out
✗ Never use exclamation points

### Sector 1 Cliffhanger
"The pattern of changes all points to a single source. Somewhere deeper in the ship. In the section the crew hasn't reached yet. Where the temperature is still dropping. Where the atoms themselves seem to be behaving according to rules that shouldn't exist. Get some rest, crew. Things are about to get stranger."

### Sector 2 Cliffhanger
"The research lab contained something else. In the back, sealed in a secondary containment unit — the original crew's final research notes. I have begun reading them. I am not going to tell you what they say yet. But the temperature anomaly was not the only experiment running on this ship. The next system — connected to how this ship stores and transfers energy — may be more significant than anything we have found so far. Get some rest. Sector 3 begins with the ship's energy systems."

### Sector 3 Content Needed
**Topic:** Energy — Kinetic & Potential · **Standard:** 6.P4U2.5 · **~10 days**
**Savvas:** Energy Transfer module, Topic 1 Lesson 2

**Key concepts:**
- Objects store energy based on chemical composition, movement, temperature, gravitational field
- Kinetic energy = motion energy (proportional to mass, grows with square of speed)
- Potential energy = stored energy (gravitational, chemical, nuclear, elastic)
- Technology allows humans to store and use energy

**Vocab:** potential energy (GPE, chemical, nuclear, elastic), kinetic energy (radiant, sound, mechanical, thermal, electrical)

**Story hook:** The original crew's research notes reveal a second experiment — something connected to how the ship stores and transfers energy that shouldn't be possible. GAIA believes the experiment is still running somewhere in the power grid. The crew must understand kinetic and potential energy to find it and understand what it was trying to do.

**Graph focus:** Introduce pie charts — parts of a whole. Energy distribution across ship systems is a natural fit (what percentage of remaining power goes to life support vs. lab vs. propulsion).

## CAMPAIGN 2: THE LIGHTHOUSE AT THE EDGE OF THE SOLAR SYSTEM
**File:** `Lighthouse.html` · **Subject:** Earth & Space Science · **~13-14 weeks Q2-Q3**
**Status:** ⬜ Not yet built

### The Story
A faint repeating signal has been detected from the outer edge of the solar system — coming from a structure that shouldn't exist. The class is the crew of the *Meridian*, a long-range science vessel sent to investigate. The journey takes them through the solar system itself — past planets, through gravitational fields, navigating eclipses, seasons, tides, and moon phases along the way. When they reach the Lighthouse, what they find raises more questions than it answers.

**Connection to Campaign 1:** The Lighthouse has been transmitting for longer than anyone expected. When the crew finally decodes its signal, it contains data that traces back to the Prometheus experiment. The signal is not a coincidence — it's a consequence. The thermal transfer device on the Prometheus sent a pulse of energy outward. The Lighthouse detected it. What the Lighthouse has been transmitting ever since is a record of what that pulse did to the solar system.

### Sector Arc (5 acts)
- **Sector 1 — Launch & Navigation:** Plotting a course. Scale is the first problem. First gravitational decisions.
- **Sector 2 — The Inner System:** Passing through the Sun-Earth-Moon system. Navigation requires understanding orbits, moon phases, tides, eclipses.
- **Sector 3 — Seasons & The Long Dark:** Solar radiation varies dramatically. Understanding Earth's tilt and energy distribution becomes critical.
- **Sector 4 — The Lighthouse:** Arrival. The structure is ancient, automated, still functioning. What it's been transmitting connects to everything.
- **Sector 5 — Home:** The return journey. A warming anomaly in Earth's atmosphere is getting worse. The greenhouse effect isn't just a concept — it's what they're flying back into.

### Stats (Campaign 2)
| Stat | Icon | What it tracks |
|---|---|---|
| Navigation Accuracy | 🧭 | How precisely on course. Gravity wells and poor decisions affect this. |
| Solar Power | ☀️ | Energy collected from Sun. Changes based on distance from Sun. |
| Signal Clarity | 📡 | How well crew can read and transmit data. |
| Earth Status | 🌍 | Condition of Earth's atmosphere. Only goes one direction unless crew intervenes. |

### Poison Pill Moment (Campaign 2)
The Lighthouse is transmitting data that could help solve the greenhouse crisis — but downloading it all will drain Solar Power to critical levels. The crew might not make it home. What do you prioritize: the data or the journey?

### GAIA's Role (Campaign 2)
GAIA comes with the crew on the Meridian. She was recovered from the Prometheus and reinstalled. She knows more about what the Lighthouse signal means than she is saying. She feeds the crew information strategically — enough to investigate, not enough to short-circuit the discovery.

## CAMPAIGN 3: THE VANISHING
**File:** `Vanishing.html` · **Subject:** Life Science · **~13-14 weeks Q3-Q4**
**Status:** ⬜ Not yet built

### The Story
Something is wrong with the most biodiverse ecosystem on Earth. Species are disappearing in a pattern no one can explain. The class is a team of field ecologists deployed to investigate a collapsing ecosystem. They must map energy flow, track matter cycles, identify predator-prey relationships, and determine whether what's happening is natural disruption — or something humans set in motion. The answer, when it comes, is both.

**Connection to Campaigns 1 & 2:** The ecosystem collapse is not random. The energy pulse from the Prometheus experiment propagated through the solar system (Campaign 2) and eventually affected Earth's energy systems. GAIA, now monitoring Earth from orbit, has been tracking the cascade. The "human impact" the crew investigates in Campaign 3 is partly the direct result of a chain of events that started on the Prometheus. The kids have been investigating this event all year without knowing they were connecting the dots.

**The end-of-year reveal moment:** In the final days of Campaign 3, GAIA tells the crew everything. She has had the full picture since the research notes in Sector 2 of Campaign 1. She chose to let each crew discover the pieces in sequence because, as she explains: "Science works this way. You rarely know you are solving the big problem until you have solved enough small ones to see the shape of it."

### Sector Arc (5 acts)
- **Sector 1 — Arrival & Assessment:** Map the food web. Build it as a living document because it's going to change.
- **Sector 2 — The Web Breaks:** A keystone species has vanished. Trace the cascade effect.
- **Sector 3 — Following the Matter:** Where are the nutrients going? First human fingerprints appear in the data.
- **Sector 4 — The Human Variable:** Evidence of human activity. The disruption isn't one thing — it's decades of decisions. Intervene or document?
- **Sector 5 — The Decision + GAIA's Reveal:** Final recommendation. Then GAIA tells them everything.

### Stats (Campaign 3)
| Stat | Icon | What it tracks |
|---|---|---|
| Biodiversity Index | 🌿 | Species variety still present. Hardest to recover once dropped. |
| Water Quality | 💧 | Watershed health. Affects every organism. |
| Food Supply Stability | 🌾 | Energy flow through food web. |
| Community Trust | 👥 | How much local human community supports the crew's work. |

### Poison Pill Moment (Campaign 3)
Removing the invasive species helps biodiversity — but the method available also harms native species temporarily. The ecosystem might get worse before it gets better. And the community is watching. Do you act now or wait for a cleaner solution that may never come?

## THE STAT SYSTEM (ALL CAMPAIGNS)

Stats update ONLY during the REVEAL screen (next class day). Never immediately after a choice.

**Warning threshold:** stat below 30% — GAIA flags it.
**Crisis threshold:** stat hits 0%, or two stats below 20% simultaneously.
**Failure tiers:** (1) forensics + checkpoint reset, (2) choices restricted, (3) GAIA narrows to 2 options with dry commentary.

**Section colors — MUST be unique, MUST be consistent across all graphs:**
- Section A: #00ff88 (green)
- Section B: #00c8ff (cyan)
- Section C: #ff3355 (red — always danger/dropping)
- Section D: #aa66ff (purple)
- Section E: #7be3ff (light blue)
- Section F: #ff8800 (orange — always warning/rising)

**CRITICAL:** Bar graphs showing ship sections must use section colors, NOT status colors (safe/caution/warning/danger). Status colors cause multiple sections to share the same color. In a classroom dry run, students thought matching colors meant the same category. Always use unique section colors.

## DAILY CLASSROOM FLOW

Same structure every day — critical for neurodiverse students.

**Choice Days (⚡):** Scenario → Exit Ticket (timer) → Discussion (timer) → Final Vote
**Lesson Days (📖):** Scenario → Reading Timer
**Graph Days (📊):** Scenario → Work Timer
**Climax Days (🏁):** Scenario only

**Three-day pedagogy cycle (confirmed from classroom testing):**
- Choice Day = first read of the lesson (reading with purpose — to find evidence for the vote)
- Deepen Day = workbook questions from Savvas student consumable
- Graph Day = Savvas interactives on Realize platform

**After vote:** Commit → Processing screen (GAIA cliffhanger, stats hidden) → Save → Home.
**Next class:** Launch → auto-routes to REVEAL → stats update live → mission.

**Cliffhanger protection:** Next Day blocked when pendingDeltas exist. GAIA cycles:
- "Results pending. I'd recommend finding out what happened before creating new things to regret."
- "The crew voted. Something happened. You haven't looked yet. Perhaps do that first."

**On reading GAIA transmissions:** Teacher reads aloud early in year for dramatic effect. Students read silently once they know the world. Calling on a student to read GAIA's lines also works.

**THE INTRO VIDEO IS NON-NEGOTIABLE.** Kids who haven't seen it lack emotional investment. Day 0 (even 20 minutes) must be dedicated to the NotebookLM video and dashboard reveal before any content day. Everything lands differently with context.

**NotebookLM intro video:** Built using three source documents (Mission Briefing, GAIA Personal Log, What The Crew Must Know). Anime style. Generated via NotebookLM Video Overview. Teacher has the downloaded video file. If regeneration needed: same three documents, anime visual style, steering prompt emphasizing GAIA as warm-but-dry AI, crew = the class, end on cliffhanger.

**On absent students:** Screenshot the four key elements (GAIA transmission, scenario, choices, exit ticket prompt) or print as one-page PDF. Student completes Google Form CER independently. Future build: "Print Day Summary" button.

Students thought matching colors meant same category. Fix: use section color scheme for ALL bar graphs showing ship sections. Section colors must be unique per section.

**Priority 4 — Graph backgrounds (only if font fix doesn't solve it)**
Dark background with light text is hard to read when projected. Discussed solution: graph panels only get a light/white background with dark text and axis labels. Rest of app stays dark. This is a toggle-based approach — try font size fix first, only add the light background if still needed after testing. Do NOT change the overall app dark theme.

## APP FEATURES (DO NOT CHANGE ENGINE)

- **localStorage** — survives browser close, same machine
- **Three-class tracking** — separate stat/branch/day state per class
- **Pending deltas** — stats frozen until reveal screen
- **Processing screen** — GAIA cliffhanger after commit, no stat reveal
- **Reveal screen** — dramatic live stat update, consequence text
- **Forensics screen** — CER framework, decision timeline, failure tiers
- **Day navigator** — dropdown shows ALL sectors and days, jump anywhere instantly
- **Presentation mode** — full-width center + horizontal stats bar visible to class
- **Day-type banner** — color coded, shows day type + day number of total
- **Teacher notes panel** — right panel only, hidden in presentation mode
- **Show Answer Graph** — dataset comparison days, reveals all answers after discussion
- **GAIA block messages** — cycling dry humor, blocks Next Day when reveal pending

## ASSESSMENT APPROACH

**In-campaign:** CER exit ticket every choice day (Google Form, daily grade). Branch extinction forensics uses CER framework built in.

**External (Canvas):** Use GAIA's voice. Same content, same rigor, different feel.
- Instead of: "What is kinetic energy?"
- GAIA asks: "Mission Control needs you to explain why Section F's hull material began to soften. Use what you know about particle speed, thermal energy, and melting points to make your case."

End of sector: Savvas lesson review (low stakes). End of unit: Savvas test or CBAS (official grade). Graph days: graph questions serve as informal cross-curricular math assessment.
